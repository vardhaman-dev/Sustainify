# ♻️ Sustainify

Sustainify is a community-driven platform focused on sustainability and social impact. It enables users to sell, donate, rent, or recycle items, discover NGOs, and promote local eco-conscious businesses — all while reducing waste and supporting a circular economy.

---

## 🚀 Why Sustainify?

Every year, millions of usable items are discarded. Sustainify bridges the gap between waste and need by connecting people who want to:

- Sell second-hand goods  
- Donate items to NGOs  
- Rent rarely-used products  
- Recycle responsibly  
- Promote eco-friendly local businesses  

Whether you’re an individual, NGO, or business — Sustainify empowers you to make a difference.

## 🌱 Vision

We aim to make reuse and donation as accessible and intuitive as buying something new — all while supporting verified NGOs and small local businesses.

Future goals include:

- Wallet or points system for donors  
- NGO verification panel  
- Map filtering with Leaflet or Mapbox  
- Real-time messaging/chat  
- Sustainability leaderboard
